Our story

A.RSM Training Centre Inc. runs forklift and equipment courses from Unit 13 at 60 Nugget Ave in Scarborough. Public sources describe the school as one of the earlier equipment and truck training schools in the Greater Toronto Area, operating under earlier names before A.RSM.

The course list covers the machines and tickets that GTA employers ask for most: counterbalance, reach truck, order picker and walkie rider forklifts, skid-steers, aerial work platforms and scissor lifts, plus fall protection, WHMIS 2015 and Transportation of Dangerous Goods.

A.RSM's own Facebook and YouTube posts introduce "our head trainer Shawn", and reviewers name the instructors and front-office staff they worked with.

How we teach

Practical first

Operators learn by driving, inspecting and loading real equipment, supported by the theory you need to answer an auditor's questions.

Straight pricing

The price of every course is published on this site. What the price includes is spelled out on each course page.

Written for employers too

A certificate is only useful if you can produce it. Employers get clear information on what to keep on file and when to renew.

How it works, start to finish

  1. 1

    Choose the course

    Use the course finder or call us. If you are not sure which class your job needs, ask your supervisor which machine you will operate.

  2. 2

    Book a date

    Call (416) 297-5435 or send the booking form. We confirm the date and what to bring.

  3. 3

    Train on the equipment

    Theory, a walk-around inspection, then supervised driving or operation.

  4. 4

    Get your certificate

    You receive your certificate when you complete the course.

  5. 5

    Renew on time

    Forklift certificates are commonly renewed every three years. We list the renewal steps on the recertification page.

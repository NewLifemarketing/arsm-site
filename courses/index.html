What is included, and what is not

Each course is priced per person. Six of the nine may carry an additional $75 fee for practical training and wallet-card creation, according to the old site.

Ontario has no forklift licence. Employers must ensure workers are competent and adequately trained. Our certificates show you completed training; your employer decides what else they need, including site-specific familiarization.

Not included: Ontario's approved Working at Heights program (our Fall Protection course is not that program), site-specific WHMIS training from your employer, and the TDG certificate, which your employer issues. Safety gear such as boots and helmets is sold separately.

Frequently asked questions

Which course should I take first?

Take the course for the machine you will actually operate. A forklift certificate does not show you can run a reach truck or order picker, and a skid-steer course does not cover a scissor lift. If your job uses several, book them in order of what you will use first. Ask your employer which they need.

Are the prices final?

The prices shown are the listed per-person prices. Some old-site pages mention an extra $75 for practical training and wallet-card creation. We have flagged it and are confirming which courses it applies to. Call (416) 297-5435 before you book if the total matters.

Do I need a licence to operate a forklift in Ontario?

There is no provincial forklift licence. Employers must ensure workers are competent and adequately trained under the Occupational Health and Safety Act. Training and a certificate from a school like ours is what most employers ask to see, alongside their own site instruction.

Can I take more than one course in a day?

Sometimes, with the short courses such as WHMIS and TDG. Machine courses take more time and concentration. Tell us what you need and we will suggest a schedule.

What languages do you teach in?

The school's website says instruction is available in English, Punjabi, Mandarin, Cantonese, Hindi and Tamil. Several reviewers mention that all languages are available. Availability depends on the instructor, so tell us your language when you book.

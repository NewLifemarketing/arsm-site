Ajax has a compact but busy economy. Employers cluster in the industrial areas along Westney Road South, the Bayly Street and Salem Road corridor, and Notion Road, with smaller commercial strips along Kingston Road West and near Harwood Avenue. The mix leans toward auto parts and service, light manufacturing, food and beverage distribution, and general warehousing. Many of these are small to mid-sized operations rather than giant distribution centres, so a single forklift or two per company is common, and operators often wear several hats. Several units are leased by tradespeople who use them as combined shop and storage space.

Residential growth in Ajax has also driven construction. Newer communities in the north and northwest, plus infill and renovation in older neighbourhoods like Pickering Village and Riverside, keep trades and contractors active. Landscapers and small excavation companies use skid-steers, and framers and finishers work from scissor lifts. Because Ajax is a commuter town, plenty of residents also work elsewhere in Durham or in Toronto, so they may look for training that sits close to a highway. Many contractors here work on a mix of new builds and older homes near Old Ajax, and the equipment they need changes from job to job. Landscape companies often keep one skid-steer running from spring through fall.

Our Scarborough centre is about 25 to 35 minutes from Ajax in normal traffic. Most people take Hwy 401 west to Markham Road or Neilson Road and then Sheppard Avenue East to Nugget Ave. Common courses for Ajax workers are counterbalance forklift for the local shops, reach truck and order picker for those moving into larger distribution jobs, and skid-steer, scissor lift and fall protection for contractors. WHMIS 2015 suits almost anyone in a workplace with cleaning products or shop chemicals, and TDG fits the drivers and receiving staff handling regulated freight.

Ontario employers must ensure workers are adequately trained and supervised for the equipment they use. In a smaller Ajax shop, that can be easy to overlook, because the person who runs the forklift may have learned informally. A documented course gives both the worker and the employer something on paper. If you have several people to train, call (416) 297-5435 and ask about a group session. Asking whether we can train at your Ajax site is fine, but we cannot promise availability until we have spoken with you. Even one operator counts.

Training from Ajax: questions

How far is A.RSM from Ajax?

Expect about 25 to 35 minutes by car in normal traffic. Take Hwy 401 west to Markham Road or Neilson Road, then Sheppard Avenue East to Nugget Ave in Scarborough. The 401 slows at rush hour, so leave a buffer on the morning of your course.

Do Ajax employers need forklift certification?

Ontario employers must ensure workers are adequately trained for the equipment they operate, including forklifts. Ajax shops along Westney Road and Notion Road, for example, often run at least one truck. Ask your employer what they require, then call (416) 297-5435 to pick a course.

Can a group from Ajax train together?

You can ask about a group. Small Ajax employers often send two or three workers at once. Call (416) 297-5435 to discuss numbers and timing. Whether training at your own site is possible is a question for us directly, and we make no promises until we confirm it with you.

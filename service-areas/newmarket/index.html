Newmarket is the largest town in northern York Region, and it acts as the commercial hub for the surrounding area. Its industrial base is strongest along Harry Walker Parkway, which runs through a large business park close to Hwy 404 and Davis Drive, with warehouses, manufacturing shops and distributors. Mulock Drive and the Leslie Street and Green Lane area add more employment land, while Yonge Street and Davis Drive carry the town's retail and services. Southlake Regional Health Centre and the surrounding medical-supply businesses add another layer. Retail head offices and call centres are also nearby, though they use little lift equipment.

Because Newmarket sits at the top of Hwy 404 and close to Hwy 400, it works well for companies serving northern York, Simcoe and Muskoka. Many warehouses supply retailers and contractors across that wider area. Forklift work is a normal part of these operations, with counterbalance trucks in most shops and reach trucks or order pickers where racking is deep. Newmarket is also a growing town, with steady residential and commercial construction, so scissor lifts, skid-steers and fall protection training come up often among local trades. The Upper Canada Mall area and the Davis Drive corridor also generate steady deliveries.

The drive to Scarborough runs about 40 to 55 minutes in normal traffic, mostly by Hwy 404 south to Hwy 401 east and then Markham Road and Sheppard Avenue East. Newmarket workers who commute south for their day jobs already know the 404 well, and its slowdowns; a mid-morning start is much easier than a rush-hour one. Most requests we would expect from Newmarket are for counterbalance forklift, reach truck, order picker and scissor lift. WHMIS 2015 is relevant for almost any employer, and TDG is useful for shipping and receiving staff, especially at medical and industrial suppliers.

Ontario employers must ensure workers are adequately trained and supervised on the equipment they use. That duty covers a Harry Walker Parkway warehouse with dozens of operators just as it covers a small contractor. Employers should know who is trained on what and when renewal is due. If you plan to send several workers from Newmarket, call (416) 297-5435 and ask about a group. On-site training at your Newmarket location is worth raising with us, though we cannot promise it until we have confirmed the details. A phone call is the quickest way to settle that.

Training from Newmarket: questions

How far is A.RSM from Newmarket?

Roughly 40 to 55 minutes by car in normal traffic. The usual route is Hwy 404 south to Hwy 401 east, then Markham Road north and Sheppard Avenue East to Nugget Ave. Expect longer at rush hour when the 404 backs up.

Do Newmarket employers need forklift certification?

Ontario employers must ensure workers are adequately trained for the equipment they use, and forklifts are a standard example. Newmarket's Harry Walker Parkway warehouses and machine shops rely on trained operators. Call (416) 297-5435 if you are not sure which course fits your job.

Can a group from Newmarket train together?

You can ask about a group. Newmarket employers sometimes send several warehouse workers on one day. Call (416) 297-5435 to discuss numbers and timing. Training at your own site is a question to ask us directly, and nothing is promised until it is confirmed.
